What ClassiFido does

ClassiFido gathers the schedules and assignments your student's teachers publish and puts them in one weekly view. We read the documents and calendars your school and its teachers make available, and we assemble them. We don't create the content — teachers do.

What that means in practice

We show you what teachers publish. If a teacher hasn't posted this week's work, or posts it somewhere we can't read, it won't appear. We'll tell you when we couldn't read something rather than pretending there was nothing there.

We can be wrong. We read documents written for humans, not for software. Sometimes a teacher changes a format and we misread it. ClassiFido is a convenience, not the system of record — your school's own tools are.

Don't rely on us alone for something that matters. If a test date or an assignment is important, check it against the source.
